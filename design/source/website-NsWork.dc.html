FlyConnect: a complete travel booking platform, built end to end.

A public booking website for customers and a B2B dashboard for staff, agents and sub-agents for a UK travel business, with live Amadeus flights, Stripe payments, finance tools and reporting.

The brief

Sell flights online to customers, and give staff, agents and sub-agents one system for bookings, payments, finance and reporting.

What we delivered

Two portals on one platform: a public booking website connected live to Amadeus, and a B2B dashboard with role-based access, payment links, finance tools and 14 report types.

What we built

Two portals, one platform.

Customer booking website

B2C · public site

Flight search: one-way, return and multi-cityResults with filters and flexible datesFlight details, baggage allowance and fare rulesPassenger details and Stripe checkoutBooking confirmation, e-ticket and emailsManage booking and cancellationHoliday packages, help and support pagesCustomer accounts with email verification

B2B dashboard

Staff, agents and sub-agents

KPI dashboard and Amadeus flight search for staffBookings: calendar, tracking, queues and group bookingsPost-booking requests: void, change and enquiryStaff, branches, roles, discounts and couponsAgents and sub-agents with their own accessPayment links, admin fees and airline blackoutsRefunds, credit notes, invoices and ledger14 report types and real-time chat

Key features

What makes it work.

Live Amadeus search and booking

Live fares, pricing and booking through the Amadeus API.

Price check before payment

Fare and availability confirmed again before the card is charged.

Stripe checkout and payment links

Customers pay online; staff and agents send payment links.

Agent and sub-agent portals

Each agent works in their own space, with their own bookings.

Role-based permissions

Super-admin, staff, agent and sub-agent roles, with permissions per module.

Group bookings and queues

Group requests, booking queues and a booking calendar.

Refunds, invoices and ledger

Cancellations, refunds, credit notes, invoices and accounts.

Real-time chat

Staff and agents chat live inside the dashboard.

PDF e-tickets and emails

E-tickets, receipts and booking emails generated automatically.

14 report types

Sales, bookings, refunds, and staff and agent performance.

Booking flow

Search to e-ticket, in six steps.

Payment is taken only after the fare is confirmed, and the booking is registered with the GDS straight after.

  1. 1

    Search

    Live fares from Amadeus, with filters and flexible dates.

  2. 2

    Price check

    Fare and availability confirmed again before payment.

  3. 3

    Passengers

    Traveller details captured and checked.

  4. 4

    Payment

    Stripe checkout, or a payment link sent by staff.

  5. 5

    Confirm

    Booking registered with the GDS.

  6. 6

    E-ticket

    PDF e-ticket and confirmation email sent automatically.
