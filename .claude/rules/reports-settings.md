## Reports and settings (backend)
- **Reports** (`/team/reports`, `reports.view`: super admins, admins, managers) are worked out from the data, in UK months and weeks:
  - **Avg. first response:** arriving to the first team reply, for first replies this month (the dashboard's rule), against last month ("18% faster than October").
  - **Avg. resolution time:** arriving to Resolved, for requests resolved this month ("2.6 days", hours under a day), against last month ("0.4 days faster").
  - **Resolved this month** ("+6 vs last month"); **Reopened requests:** requests moved from Resolved or Closed back to In Progress this month (status history), shown as a share of resolved ("5% of resolved"), as in the design.
  - **Requests this week:** sent each day, Monday to Sunday (UK). **Requests by project:** the six projects with the most requests, all time. **Developer performance:** each active developer's requests sent this month, resolved this month and average first response this month.
  - **Downloads** (`reports.view`, like the page; `reports/report-export.service.ts`): `GET /team/reports/export.pdf|xlsx|csv` with the page's own `from`, `to`, `customer`, `developer`. PDF (pdfkit, A4, navy band with the logo from `report-logo.ts`, tiles, tables, page numbers; text outside Western European letters is simplified for the built-in fonts), Excel (exceljs, a sheet per section: Summary, This week, Projects, Developers), CSV (one file, sections one after another). Each download: audit `export.downloaded` (`report-pdf` and so on). Buttons: the **Export** menu beside the report title (`ReportExport` in `components/team/report-filters.tsx`, built on `MenuButton` from `ui/row-menu.tsx`): Download PDF, Download Excel, Download CSV, Print.
  - **Kept ready:** a report for a range that is already over is kept for 10 minutes per app process (`ReportsService.finished`, at most 50); the "this week" chart is always live, and this month (or any range up to today) is always worked out afresh.
  - The backend sends the words; a change for the better shows green, for the worse red.
  - **Choices (Reports v2):** `?from=&to=` (UK dates, both included, up to 400 days; `BAD_RANGE` otherwise), `?customer=`, `?developer=`. With dates, every number covers that range and compares with the same number of days just before ("vs the period before"); the cards say "Resolved" instead of "Resolved this month", and Requests by project counts the range (`projectsAllTime: false`). Without choices the page is exactly the designed month view. A customer or developer narrows every number. `sla`: Reply SLA met (first replies in the period before `dueAt`) and Fix SLA met (resolved in the period before `resolveDueAt`; requests without a fix clock not counted). `filters` lists the customers and developers. The page's choices form is a plain GET form (`ReportFilters`: From, To, Customer, Developer, Apply, and "This month" to clear them); the form and the Export menu are hidden in print.
- **Scheduled jobs (cron health):** `cli/run-job.ts` notes each run in `settings` (key `job:<name>`: time, ok, what it did; `jobs/job-runs.ts`). `GET /team/settings/jobs` (super admins) gives each job of `JOB_SCHEDULE` with `late` (daily jobs after 26 hours, the weekly after 8 days, send-emails after 30 minutes); the Settings page shows it (`components/team/scheduled-jobs.tsx`).
- **Settings** (`/team/settings`, `settings.manage`: super admins only): the four response targets, the four fix targets (`resolutionTargets`), the bank holidays (`slaCalendar.bankHolidays`, "YYYY-MM-DD", real dates only, sorted, England and Wales 2026-2027 by default), the reopen window (`requestRules.reopenWithinDays`, 1 to 365, default 14) and the four switches, saved together (the three newer parts are optional in the body). Targets are checked with the SLA's own `parseTarget()`, up to 30 business days. Each setting that changed gets a `settings.changed` audit entry with the old and new value. **New targets apply to new requests and to priority changes; requests already open keep their due time.**
