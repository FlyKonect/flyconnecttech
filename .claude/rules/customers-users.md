## Customers and users (backend)
- **Customers:** managers may look (`customers.view`); only super admins and admins add customers and projects, change a project's status, resend invitations and send reset links (`customers.manage`). Developers get nothing ("not found" in the website).
- **Add customer** goes through `InvitationsService.inviteUser()` with the projects: the customer, its contact (Invited) and the projects (codes from `nextCode`) are saved in one transaction, then the invitation email lists the projects. A failed email keeps the customer; the page says to use Resend invitation. An email already used by another customer account answers `EMAIL_TAKEN` (a team account with the same email does not count).
- **A customer's status** is its contact person's (Invited, Active, Disabled).
- **Users:** super admins and admins only. Who may add, resend to or disable whom follows `canInviteRole()`: admins never act on a super admin. `GET /team/users/roles` gives the Add user screen the roles this person may give and the permission table from `policies.ts`.
- **Disable user:** status Disabled, every session deleted, open invitations and reset links stop working, audit entry. Not yourself. Their open work is released in the same transaction (`AccountDeletionService.releaseWork()`, shared with Delete): open requests go to `transferTo` (body, optional: an active developer, not them; otherwise 409 `NOT_A_DEVELOPER`), with the line "Transferred from A to B by Y (A's account was disabled)" and one notification to B, or, with nobody chosen, back to Under Review unassigned ("Unassigned because A's account was disabled"); open leads they owned and demos they hosted go back to nobody. The audit entry lists the request codes. The Disable dialog shows the count and "Who takes over".
- **Enable user** (`POST /team/users/:id/enable`, same limits): Active again if they had activated (a `credential` account exists), otherwise Invited (their old link stays revoked: Resend invitation). Audit `user.enabled`.
- **Reset two-step sign-in** (`users.resetTwoFactor`: super admins only; `POST /team/users/:id/two-factor-reset`, same limits): removes their `twoFactor` row, sets `twoFactorEnabled` false, signs them out everywhere, audit `user.twoFactorReset`. At the next sign-in the guard sends them to the two-step setup, as on their first day. In the Users "⋯" menu for active people.
- **Change role** (`POST /team/users/:id/role`, same limits as Add user for both the old and the new role, never yourself): applies from their next click. Only developers hold requests, so a change from or to Developer runs `releaseWork()` ("Unassigned because A is no longer a developer"; leads they owned go back to nobody). Audit `user.roleChanged` with from and to. The "⋯" menu offers it with the roles this person may give.
- **Change email** (`POST /team/users/:id/email`, the same `mayManage` check as resend; `accounts/email-change.service.tsx`): an invited person's email changes at once and a new invitation goes there (the old link stops working); an active person gets a confirmation link at the new address (stored hashed in `verifications` as `email-change:<hash>`, 48 hours, once; a new request replaces the old). The public page `/confirm-email/[token]` (`GET`/`POST /accounts/email-change/:token[/confirm]`) changes nothing on opening; its button confirms: `email` and `loginEmail` change, every session ends, the old address gets a notice, audit `user.emailChanged` (by the admin who asked). Refused: an email used by another account on the same side (`EMAIL_TAKEN`), a disabled account (enable it first). Buttons: Change email in the Users "⋯" menu and on the customer page (`components/team/change-email-dialog.tsx`).
- **Edit customer and project** (`customers.manage`): `POST /team/customers/:id/details` (company name; the country shown is the contact's own, from their profile) and `POST /team/customers/:id/projects/:projectId` (name and type), with audits `customer.updated` and `project.updated`. Buttons: Edit company in the customer header, Edit on each project row (`customer-actions.tsx`).
- **Export (Excel or CSV)** (`exports.download`: super admins and admins; `common/csv.ts` and `common/table-file.ts`): `GET /team/requests/export.csv|xlsx`, `/team/customers/export.csv|xlsx`, `/team/leads/{demos|quotes|messages}/export.csv|xlsx` (any other ending: 400 `BAD_FORMAT`), with the list's own filters (requests and customers read the list's pages, so permissions and filters are the same; leads have their own query, with emails, `exportTable()`). Up to 10,000 rows. CSV: every cell quoted, formula-like cells (`= + - @`) prefixed with an apostrophe, UTF-8 with a byte order mark, UK times. Excel (exceljs): one sheet, navy header row, frozen and filterable, cells written as values (never formulas). Each download gets an audit entry `export.downloaded` (with `format`). The button is the **Export** menu (`components/team/export-menu.tsx`, the same `MenuButton` as Reports; it copies the address's filters, not the page): Download Excel, Download CSV.
- **Delete user / Delete customer** (`users.delete`, `customers.delete`: super admins and admins; same limits as Disable; `accounts/account-deletion.service.ts`): the account goes for good: status `DELETED`, sessions, password, two-factor, invitation and reset links removed, personal details and notifications erased, `email`/`loginEmail` replaced by a private address so the real email is free, the old one kept in `deletedEmail`. The row stays so history keeps its author, its name marked "(deleted)" (`deleted-marks.ts`). A deleted team member's open requests go back to Under Review unassigned (with a line), and open leads/demos they owned or hosted go back to nobody. A deleted customer leaves the lists (`customers.deletedAt`, name marked "(deleted)") and its open requests are closed with a line (no email). Lists and lookups never show deleted accounts. **Adding the same email again on the same side** answers `PREVIOUS_ACCOUNT` (409, with name, date and counts) until the request says `previous: "restore"` (the same row comes back, with its history, customer, projects and requests, and a new invitation) or `"fresh"` (a new account; the old one is not offered again). The screens ask with `PreviousAccountDialog` (`components/team/previous-account-dialog.tsx`).

## Projects (backend `src/projects/`, team `/team/projects`, portal `/portal/projects`)
- **Who:** managers look (`projects.view`); super admins and admins add and change (`projects.manage`); delete and restore is `projects.delete` (super admins, admins). Developers see only the projects they are on (`projectScope()`; any other answers 404; see "Modules, the project team and discussions" below). Customers only through the portal, within `customerScope()` (another customer's project or file: 404).
- **Rules** (`project-rules.ts`, the website's copy in `lib/projects.ts` and `lib/team/forms.ts`): "What's included" is one item a line (`itemsFromLines()`: blanks and bullets dropped, up to 50 items of 160 characters); `progressOf()` is the share of items Done (no items: no percentage); dates are calendar days "YYYY-MM-DD" saved at midnight UTC and sent back with `calendarDay()`; addresses must be http/https; the target date can't be before the start; the lead must be an active team member (`NOT_A_TEAM_MEMBER`).
- **Team routes** (`team-projects.controller.ts`): `GET /team/projects` (paged; `?q=`, `?status=planning|in-design|in-development|live|completed|deleted`, `?customer=`; summary counts per status and deleted), `GET /team/projects/choices` (customers and leads, for Add project), `POST /team/projects` (`project.added`), `GET /team/projects/:code` (with `teamNotes`, `leadChoices`, `can`), `POST …/details` (every field; `project.updated` with the changed field names), `POST …/status` (`project.statusChanged`), items (`POST …/items` with `lines`, `…/items/:id` title/detail/status (sets or clears `doneAt`), `…/move` up/down, `…/remove`), updates (`POST …/updates` multipart `body` and up to 5 `files`, `…/updates/:id` edits the text without a new email, `…/remove` deletes it and its files from disk with `project.updateRemoved`), `GET …/files/:id` (download, nosniff), `POST …/delete` and `…/restore`. The customer page's own project routes (add, name and type, status) still work.
- **Item changes are not logged or sent;** they show in the portal at once. Only updates notify the customer (in-app kind `project` and the email, `project-events.service.tsx`).
- **Delete project:** 409 `HAS_OPEN_REQUESTS` (with `open`) while requests are open; no requests and no updates: removed for good (items cascade); otherwise `deletedAt`/`deletedById` set and the name marked with `markDeleted()`. Audit `project.deleted` (`forGood`). Restore takes the mark off (`project.restored`). A deleted project is read-only on its team page (`can.manage` false; changes answer 404), and is left out of the customer page, the Customers list's project names and count, the portal (`GET /portal/projects`, also New request's choices; a new request on it answers 404), the request page's project choices and the invitation email's project list. Old requests keep showing its name with "(deleted)".
- **Team notes** (`teamNotes`) are only in the team route's answer: the portal's `pageSelect` never asks for them (tested in `projects`), and the data export's project select leaves them out.
- **Files:** `FileStorageService.keepIn(files, 'projects/<id>')`; the upload limits come from `uploads/upload-multer.ts` (shared with requests).
- **Tested in** `test/db/projects.db-spec.ts` and `frontend/e2e/projects.spec.ts`.

## Modules, the project team and discussions (`src/projects/`)
- **Permissions:** `projects.view` (all team, within `projectScope()`: developers only on projects they are members of), `projects.manage` (super admins, admins: details, modules, items, updates, the team), `projects.work` (super admins, admins, developers: module stages and item ticks; `stagesFor()` in `module-rules.ts` limits developers to Design, Development, Testing, Retest and Client review), `projects.delete`, `discussions.join` (all team, within `projectScope()`). The project routes use these, not the customer ones.
- **Modules** (`modules.service.ts`, `module-rules.ts`, `module-writes.ts`): `POST /team/projects/:code/modules` (add), `…/modules/:id` (edit), `…/move`, `…/remove` (`project.moduleRemoved`), `…/stage` `{ stage, note? }` (`stageMove()`: a new round on Changes requested; Done sets `doneAt` and ticks the open items; leaving Done clears it; a `module_stage_changes` row each time). Client review notifies and emails the customer (`ProjectEventsService.moduleReady`). The customer's `POST /portal/projects/:code/modules/:id/review` `{ decision: 'approve' | 'changes', note }` works only in Client review (409 `NOT_IN_REVIEW`); the team hears (`moduleDecided`, `projectTeam()`: lead and members, or super admins and admins). Items: `POST …/items` takes `moduleId`; `POST …/items/:id/status` is the tick (`projects.work`); edit, move and remove stay `projects.manage`. New projects' "What's included" goes into a "General" module (`createModule()`, also used by Add customer).
- **Project team:** `POST /team/projects/:code/members` `{ userId }` and `…/members/:userId/remove` (`projects.manage`; audits `project.memberAdded` / `project.memberRemoved`; the person added gets a notice). The project page answers `members`, `memberChoices` and `can.work` / `can.viewCustomer`.
- **Discussions** (`topics.service.ts`, `topics.controller.ts`, `topic-events.service.tsx`, `topic-rules.ts`): one service, the kind fixed by the controller: `/team/projects/:code/discussions` (CUSTOMER), `/team/projects/:code/team-discussions` (INTERNAL), `/portal/projects/:code/discussions` (CUSTOMER only; any other kind answers 404). Routes: list (`?status=open|closed`, paged), create (multipart: title, body, files, moduleId, `ask`), `:number`, `:number/posts` (multipart: body, files, `proposal`, `ask`), `:number/posts/:id` (edit, own only), `:number/answer`, `:number/close`, `:number/reopen`, `:number/files/:id`; team only `:number/posts/:id/remove` and `:number/delete` (admins; `topic.deleted`); portal only `:number/posts/:id/decision`. Numbers come from `withNumber()` (retries on a clash). System lines are SYSTEM posts (`systemLine`). Asks: only people from `askChoices()`; replying sets `answeredAt`. Who hears: `participants()` and `projectTeam()`; emails by `TopicEventsService.send` (customers follow `discussionReplies`; INTERNAL never emails a customer).
- **Tested in** `test/db/modules.db-spec.ts`, `test/db/discussions.db-spec.ts` (with `test/db/support/test-app.ts`), `frontend/e2e/modules.spec.ts` and `frontend/e2e/discussions.spec.ts`.
